The Unbroken Thread

Forty days of scripture for people who almost quit

A daily reading companion. Short entries. No homework. Just a thread through the dark you can hold with one hand.

Sample chapter

Day 1 — Clay

Read 2 Corinthians 4:7–12 slowly, twice. Do not mine it for a caption. Notice that Paul is not ashamed of the cracks. The cracks are how the light is described.

Write one sentence: “Today I am a jar, not a brand.” Carry it. If you forget, that is fine. Clay is not dramatic. Clay is just true.

Sample chapter

Day 2 — How long

Psalm 13 is four movements: complaint, protest, petition, a thin layer of trust at the end. You are allowed to stop at protest. God can handle an unfinished psalm. Many of us were taught to skip to verse five because it photographs better.

Pray the first four verses until they bore you. Boredom in prayer is sometimes the nervous system standing down. That is not a failure. That is a beginning.
